Understand Damp

The Three Types of Damp

Correct diagnosis is critical — the wrong treatment wastes money and leaves the problem unsolved.

Rising Damp

Groundwater travels up through porous brickwork when the damp proof course has failed or doesn't exist — typically affecting the lower metre of walls.

  • Tide-mark staining on lower walls
  • White salt deposits on plaster
  • Peeling wallpaper near skirting boards
  • Damp smell, especially in winter
Learn More →

Penetrating Damp

Water enters the property through a defect in the building — failed pointing, cracked render, blocked gutters or faulty flashings — at any height.

  • Damp patches after heavy rain
  • Staining on ceilings or high walls
  • Spalling or crumbling brickwork
  • Localised damp in specific rooms
Learn More →

Condensation

The most common form — warm moist air hits cold surfaces and deposits water, creating the conditions for black mould. Often misdiagnosed as structural damp.

  • Black mould in corners and on ceilings
  • Streaming windows in the morning
  • Musty smell throughout the home
  • Damp worse in cold months

FAQ

Common Questions

How do I know if I have rising damp or condensation? ▾
Rising damp shows as tide-mark staining on the lower metre of walls with salt deposits. Condensation causes black mould in corners and on ceilings, worse in winter. A survey with calibrated equipment is the only reliable way to tell — our qualified surveyors will diagnose it correctly and give you a clear written report.
What does a survey involve? ▾
A qualified surveyor visits, carries out a full inspection inside and out, takes calibrated moisture readings, and gives you verbal feedback on the day. A written report with findings, photographs, and recommendations is sent to you by email within 48 hours.
Do you carry out any remedial works? ▾
No — we are a survey-only company. We survey, diagnose, and report. We don't carry out any damp proofing, plastering, or ventilation works. This means our advice is 100% independent — we have no financial incentive to recommend work that isn't needed.
Why should I get an independent survey first? ▾
Many damp proofing contractors offer "free surveys" but profit from the works they recommend. An independent survey from a company that does no works protects you from unnecessary treatment. Our report tells you exactly what's wrong — so any quotes you obtain are based on the correct specification.
What does a homebuyer damp report include? ▾
A full inspection of all accessible walls, floors and ceilings with a calibrated moisture meter; identification of any damp or timber defects; photographic evidence; written findings and diagnosis; and clear recommendations for any remedial action required. Accepted by most mortgage lenders and solicitors.
Can damp cause health problems? ▾
Yes. Persistent damp and mould can trigger or worsen asthma, allergies and respiratory infections — particularly harmful for children, the elderly and those with weakened immune systems. The NHS recommends addressing damp and mould promptly. An accurate diagnosis is the first step.
